Decision Audit Trail

We don't ask an AI to judge a case. Each institution's SOP becomes a ladder of yes/no questions per alert; a fixed rulebook turns the answers into CLOSE, RFI or ESCALATE. Every answer shows the clause, the facts and the documents behind it, and the same inputs give the same decision on every run.

Reproducibility: five runs, one answer

Each run of the whole development set is fingerprinted (a hash of the output file). Identical fingerprints mean identical verdicts, reason codes, reasons and evidence for every case.
